Dedication

Folio I

Within these leaves are bound the charms I have woven for you, born of my faith that magic is real. May these words carry that quiet power to you. They are written with all sincerity: you have made my days brighter, and I am grateful you exist in them.

☾ ✦ ☀

Charm of Athena

for wisdom & good counsel

Owl-eyed and steady, you always know the clever thing to say and truly listen when it matters. Yours to carry, today and every day after, I bind this charm of Athena to you— may her wisdom always guard your mind and light your path.

Sapientia Lucet“May your plans be sharp and your doubts be small.”

Charm of Apollo

for light & laughter

Where you walk in, the room warms up. Your humor and your creativity are a golden sun in my sky— truly, you are my sunshine. Yours to carry, today and every day after, I cast this charm of Apollo so that warmth, light, and bright laughter always return to you.

Lux Aurea“May every day hand you a reason to smile.”

Charm of Hermes

for loyalty & swift help

Whenever I need a hand, you arrive on winged sandals— a messenger of good news, a friend across every road. Yours to carry, today and every day after, I echo your own call to Lord Hermes and cast this charm so that swift help, safe travels, and loyal company always find their way back to you.

Pennae Fidei“May the road rise gently to meet your feet.”

Charm of Hestia

for warmth & home

You are a hearth-fire to everyone around you: kind, patient, and comforting. Being near you feels like home. Yours to carry, today and every day after, I cast this charm of Hestia so that peace, safety, and the same gentle warmth you give others always surround you.

Ignis Domus“May your flame never be dimmed.”

Charm of Artemis

for courage & freedom

I know within time you'll be able to chase what you want with a steady aim and a free heart. Never trade that wildness for anyone. Yours to carry, today and every day after, I cast this charm of Artemis so that fierce courage, true freedom, and an unerring aim always stay yours.

Luna Libera“May your arrow land true under every moon.”

Die letzte Seite

Thank you for being my friend. ✦ For someone who doesn't believe in nevers, I know our threads aren't done weaving together yet. Our paths will cross again— the Gods will it, and we are their favorite entertainment, after all.
